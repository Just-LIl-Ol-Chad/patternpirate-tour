One inch tall. Ten foot quilt.

Tracey is about an inch tall, on a quilt about ten feet across. She puts in every single stitch herself, with a quilting needle as tall as she is, row by row until the whole quilt is done.

Somewhere on this quilt is Tracey.10 ft across

Drag the button to zoom all the way in.

She works hard. And she loves it.

One stitch every two seconds. The path she follows is never a straight line. It is a pattern, whatever pattern PatternPirate makes.

  1. Wind up. Rises, lifts the needle high, leans back.
  2. Drive. Slams the needle into the fabric in a deep squat.
  3. Strain. Shakes while pushing. Sweat flies.
  4. Pull. Yanks the needle out, the stitch tightens, hearts pop.
  5. Step. On to the next hole. The quilt moves one stitch under her.

A ship made of quilt

Everything in her world is quilt. Star, log cabin and nine patch sails. A patchwork hull with button portholes. A quilted sea and sky. Tracey rides the bow and stitches the sea as she sails.

Lettering with a pirate accent

Headings and big words are set in Pirata One, a pirate blackletter. Big words can wear the stitched look: a solid fill with a dashed outline, like a line of quilting.

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Pp Qq Rr Ss Tt 0123456789

Small print stays in a plain, easy face like this one, so nobody has to squint at instructions in blackletter.

It must look like Tracey

People who know her should recognize her. Likeness beats style every time. Her face is locked, and nobody redraws it from scratch.

Quilting only

Everything has to read as quilting stitches in a quilt. Not general sewing, not embroidery, not cross-stitch.

The giant quilting needle is her tool

The look is its own

Tracey has her own quilting business, and that is hers. PatternPirate does not take its colors or its look from there.

Two official styles

A, the brand mascot: full color patchwork pieces with a stitched outline. J, the converter style: one navy thread on cream, like a longarm quilting file. When J moves, she is see-through, only stitches.
